Privacy Policy — 90+ Draft

Last updated: August 2026

90+ Draft ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected, who collects it, and for what purpose.

Summary

  • We do not create user accounts and do not ask for your name, email, or personal data to play.
  • We have no servers of our own that store your information: your progress, history, statistics, and collection are stored only on your device.
  • We use third-party advertising (Google AdMob) in its non-personalized mode, and offer in-app purchases processed by Apple.

Data stored on your device

Your tournament history, statistics, player collection, and preferences (formation, card theme, consumables, etc.) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to any server of ours, and we cannot see them. If you delete the app, this data is removed.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob, configured as non-personalized ads. To show them, cap their frequency, and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • Usage data (app interaction, advertising data)
  • Coarse location
  • Diagnostic data (crashes and performance)

Because the ads are non-personalized, this data is not used for cross-app tracking and is not linked to your identity. The processing of this data is governed by Google's privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

In-app purchases

The app offers a one-time purchase (Premium) and a consumables pack. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment data (card, etc.); Apple manages it according to its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at collecting personal data from children and uses non-personalized advertising.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The "last updated" date indicates the current version.
